# Looking for a Pure Writer mod APK? Please use the genuine app

If you were searching for a Pure Writer mod APK, a “Pro” mod, or a cracked copy, please download Pure Writer from the official sources on this page instead.

Mod APKs and cracked copies come from unknown sources. They can carry malware that steals your documents and personal information such as passwords and photos, and they can also corrupt your writing or cause you to lose it. We can’t guarantee the safety of your documents in a cracked version.

## The core features are free

There’s no need to take that risk. The core features of Pure Writer for Android are free to use, with no ads: the editor, History, automatic backups, cloud backup, cloud sync, and more. Preview and export, custom paper backgrounds and fonts, and some other features belong to Pure Writer Pro.

Pure Writer for iOS can be tried free for 7 days with every feature, and Pure Writer Desktop is free for now. For details, see [Can I use Pure Writer without buying Pro?](/en/manual/#is-there-a-huge-feature-loss-without-pure-writer-pro) in the user manual.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Is there a safe Pure Writer mod APK?

No. A mod APK has been altered by someone else, and there’s no way to see what was added to it. The only versions of Pure Writer we can stand behind are the ones from the official sources above: Google Play, the App Store, and this website.

### Can I download a Pure Writer APK, or an older version, from another site?

We recommend installing Pure Writer for Android from Google Play. APK files reposted on other download sites, older versions included, may have been modified, and we can’t guarantee their safety.

### Is Pure Writer for Android free?

Yes. The editor, History, automatic backups, cloud backup, cloud sync, and more are free to use, with no ads. Pure Writer Pro adds features such as preview and export, and custom paper backgrounds and fonts.
